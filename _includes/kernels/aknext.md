1. Download `{{ include.aknext_themeFile }}`.

1. Open/Extract the `.7z` archive. You can use [7-Zip](https://7-zip.org/download.html) to do this.

1. Inside the `.7z` archive, you will find a folder called `{{ include.aknext_themeFolder }}`. Copy this folder to `/_nds/akmenunext/ui` on your flashcart/console SD card.

1. Place the SD card back into your flashcart/console, and boot AKMenu-Next.

1. Press the `START` key to open the AKMenu-Next start menu popup, then select `Themes`.

1. Find the theme under the categories and press `A` to set `{{ include.aknext_themeFolder }}`, then press `X` to save, and `A` again to restart.
